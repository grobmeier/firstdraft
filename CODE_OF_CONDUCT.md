# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community.
Contributors must communicate constructively, respect differing experience and
perspectives, and focus criticism on the work rather than the person.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate
intimidation, and publishing another person's private information without
permission. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict
participation when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. The maintainer will
handle reports confidentially and respond proportionately.
